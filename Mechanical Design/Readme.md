# Mechanical Design Documentation

## Overview
The BCPSC Robot features a hybrid physical frame. Rather than an out-of-the-box kit, it is a custom-built chassis created by merging the best architectural qualities of two well-known open-source humanoid platforms: **EZ-Robot** and **InMoov**.
 
**Lead Mechanical Designer:** Farsad E Hossain

## Design Details

### 1. Hybrid 3D Printed Structure
- **Combining Platforms:** Because EZ-Robot and InMoov parts were not natively designed to fit together, the team redesigned joints, mounting points, and body proportions from scratch.
- **Iteration:** Creating a symmetrical, highly durable frame required multiple iterations of 3D modeling, test printing, and fit checking to ensure parts didn't collide.

### 2. Lower Base & Mobility
- **Weight Distribution:** The wheeled lower base was structurally designed and balanced to counter the high center of gravity generated by the humanoid upper torso and head.
- **Mobility:** Built around two DC motors with differential drive mechanisms to allow for pivoting and smooth forward motion.

### 3. Anatomical Features
- **Expressive Face:** The head houses an eye mechanism capable of panning and a jaw mechanism that physically simulates speech articulation.
- **Articulated Hand:** Features a fully assembled five-finger mechanical hand, structurally capable of performing over 20 distinct physical gestures.

## Challenges & Solutions
1. **Structural Merging:** Merging the two different platforms meant simple assembly wasn't an option. **Solution:** Complete structural redesign of the joints. Sustained rounds of physical fit testing were employed to correct imbalances before finalizing the printable files.
